Why choose CEPA?

A relationship-based study abroad partner in Europe.

CEPA brings together local European expertise, U.S.-based support, and personal service for students, families, faculty, advisors, and institutions.

People

Teams in Europe and the USA

Coordinators and program specialists support every audience with clear communication across time zones.

Service

Relationship-based service

Personal guidance keeps each program path responsive, organized, and aligned with real academic goals.

Network

Local European connections

Longstanding networks connect learning with local people, places, institutions, and communities abroad.

Place

Distinctive program settings

Programs can be rooted in memorable European places that make coursework, culture, and context tangible.

Sustainability@CEPA

CEPA is committed to positively impacting climate and social sustainability.

CEPA approaches sustainability as a shared responsibility across its operations and study abroad programs. The organization supports certified carbon-offset projects and funds nature-positive work, including native tree planting tied to participant travel and program activity.
